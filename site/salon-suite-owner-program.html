In short

RetainOS for salon suite owners is a leasing and renter retention program. Renters earn complimentary rewards, like hotel savings cards and getaway certificates, for signing a lease, renewing it and referring another pro who signs. Every renter can also use RetainOS with their own clients, as an amenity of your building. Your rent stays the same. We set everything up for you, in English and Spanish.

An empty suite costs you every week it sits.

Renters are independent pros. When their book slows down, they move, or go back to a commission salon. Then you're paying for listings, showings and weeks of empty rent to replace them.

The usual fix is a month of free rent. It fills one suite, costs a full month of income, and teaches the next renter to wait for a better deal.

The buildings that stay full do two things: they give renters a reason to sign now, and they help renters stay busy enough to stay.

Questions owners ask

Who is this for?

Owners and managers of salon suite buildings, booth-rental salons and beauty co-working spaces. You're the customer. Your renters get RetainOS for their own clients as a perk of leasing with you.

Why not just offer a month of free rent?

A month of free rent costs you a full month of income on every lease, and it teaches renters to shop for the next deal. A complimentary getaway or hotel savings card feels bigger to the renter and costs you far less than a month's rent. You can still use concessions; this gives you another option.

When is a lease reward earned?

After the lease is signed and the first payment clears. For renewals, after the renewal is signed. For renter referrals, after the referred pro signs and pays. You set the exact rules, and we put them in writing.

Do my renters have to use the loyalty program?

No. It's a perk, not a requirement. Renters who use it get visit rewards, referral tracking and reminders for their own clients, under their own business name.

What happens if a renter moves out?

Their client list and history stay theirs. They can keep RetainOS on their own plan, or stop. Nothing about your building's program changes.

Do renters pay anything for a getaway?

Yes, and we say so upfront. The stay is complimentary, but the guest pays taxes and fees for each night when activating, plus resort fees where they apply. Flights are not included. You can choose to cover the taxes and fees.

Can a franchise location use this?

Often yes, but check your franchise agreement first. Some brands control which perks a location can advertise. Independent suite buildings can start right away.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is per building and depends on how many suites you have. Per suite, it costs less than a renter would pay on their own. Book a 15-minute call and we'll send you a quote.
