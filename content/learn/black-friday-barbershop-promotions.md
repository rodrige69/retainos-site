# 3 Black Friday Barbershop Promos That Bring Clients Back Without Cheaper Cuts

A customer walks in for a holiday cut, thanks you, and reaches for their jacket. This is where your Black Friday promotion can earn its keep. Decide what you want to happen next.


- **Fill a quieter slot:** Offer the next appointment at checkout. The client pays your usual price. Your team checks availability and books the slot or gets permission to send a reminder.
- **Bring in a new client:** Offer a dining reward after a referred friend completes a paid cut. Your team verifies who sent the referral and discloses any activation fee.
- **Thank repeat clients:** Reserve a getaway certificate for a defined milestone. Your team tracks paid visits and explains what the certificate covers, what the recipient pays, and any travel restrictions.


**Short answer:** Keep the holiday cut at full price. Offer the next appointment before the customer leaves. Use a dining reward as a thank-you for a qualifying return or referral, and reserve a getaway certificate for a higher milestone your shop can afford.

## 1. Sell the next seat in the chair
Ask: “When do you usually get cleaned up again? Want me to save you a spot?” If they’d rather decide later, ask permission to text your booking link when they’re due. Example: “Hey [Name], it’s [Barber] at [Shop]. Ready for another cut? Here’s where you can grab a time: [Link].” Use your existing booking link.

## 2. Give a regular a reason to bring a friend
Try “Your next referral could earn you a dining reward.” Before promoting it, decide who qualifies, how staff credit the referral, and what the recipient actually gets. Have staff confirm the friend’s first paid cut before issuing the reward. Show any activation fee and restrictions before customers opt in.


## 3. Turn the getaway into a milestone
Make it a thank-you after a defined number of paid visits or qualifying referrals. Set a reward budget and confirm terms before announcing it. Tell customers what the certificate covers, which fees they pay, and any booking or travel restrictions. A getaway certificate is different from an all-expenses-paid trip.


## Worked Example (Illustrative)
Test a dining reward with a small group of regulars. Track qualifying referrals, completed paid cuts, and what you spent delivering rewards. Compare that with the same period or a similar group if available. Bookings and clicks are useful signals, but count paid visits when evaluating the offer.
