# 3 Black Friday Salon Offers That Protect Your Service Prices

If your prime December slots already sell at full price, pick a quieter January slot and work backward. Use priority booking if regulars want first pick, a service extra if you have time to deliver it, or a referral reward if you have capacity for new clients.


**Short answer:** Offer priority booking, a low-cost service extra, or recognition for a qualifying referral. Tie the offer to dates with capacity, spell out the terms, and track completed appointments.



See the [salon program page](https://retainos.co/hair-salon-loyalty-program) for plans, and [how to bring back clients who have not rebooked](https://retainos.co/learn/bring-back-salon-clients) for the follow-up sequence.
## 1. Give regulars first pick of January slots
Open a small set of January appointments to returning clients before your broader announcement. Share the real dates and a direct booking link. Make the message clear about who gets early access and when it ends.


## 2. Offer a small extra with a full-price visit
Choose a perk your team can deliver within the appointment, such as a conditioning treatment when it fits the existing service. Example: “Book an eligible January color appointment and we’ll include a conditioning treatment.” State which services and dates qualify. Check the product and staff time before committing.


## 3. Thank regulars for referrals that become paid visits
Give regulars a simple way to share your salon’s booking link. If you offer a thank-you, explain how the referral is attributed and when each person earns it. Count the completed paid appointment.


## Worked Example (Illustrative)
A salon has six quieter January slots and offers a $3 product-cost extra for each qualifying appointment. If all six qualify, product cost is $18, plus any staff time. Track appointments completed and total cost before repeating the offer.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Should I run all three ideas?


Start with the one that fits your capacity and is easiest to explain at checkout.
### What should I put in the offer terms?


List the booking dates, eligible appointments, reward or perk, and any real capacity limit.
