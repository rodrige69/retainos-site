# How Experience-Based Loyalty Rewards Work

Not every customer reward has to be another discount on a haircut or grooming service. Experience-based rewards offer access to lifestyle benefits—such as eligible hotel savings, dining offers, and promotional travel opportunities.

**Short answer:** A business defines qualifying paid visits or referrals, verifies that customers meet the rules, issues an eligible experience-based benefit, and provides accurate redemption instructions and terms.

## How the Process Works

First, define the milestone. Second, enroll the customer and record eligible activity. Third, verify paid visits or qualifying referrals. Fourth, issue the reward. Fifth, provide terms and instructions. Finally, distinguish issuance, activation, booking, and actual redemption.

## Hotel Savings Cards

A card advertising $100 in hotel savings is not $100 in cash, a prepaid hotel room, or a guarantee of $100 realized savings. Customers choose eligible bookings under the provider's terms and pay remaining balances and applicable charges. Rates, dates, inventory, and restrictions affect actual savings.

## Dining Rewards

These may provide discounts at participating restaurants. Participation, redemption rules, minimum purchases, and activation fees may apply. Do not call a promotional discount a fully prepaid meal.

## Getaway Certificates

These may provide promotional access to eligible resort accommodations. Activation charges, taxes, resort/nightly fees, mandatory meal plans at some all-inclusive properties, airfare, transportation, and other expenses may apply. Confirm the exact certificate's availability, deadlines, restrictions, and any presentation requirements.

## Experience Rewards vs Discounts

Discounts reduce the price of the core service. Experience-based rewards may preserve that price, but introduce reward costs and redemption complexity. Neither is universally better. Ask customers which benefits they actually want.

## Choosing and Explaining Rewards

Check relevance, cost, redemption practicality, support, and clarity. Prefer **“Earn a $100 hotel savings card toward an eligible booking; terms and booking costs apply”** over **“Get a free hotel stay.”**

## Measuring Value

Track enrollment, verified repeat visits, qualifying referrals, rewards issued, activation and redemption where available, expenses, and customer feedback. Do not confuse advertised face value with business cost or incremental revenue.

## Worked Example (Illustrative)

**Illustrative hotel example:** If an eligible booking is priced at $240 and the provider applies $60 in savings, the resulting amount is $180 before other applicable charges. This is arithmetic, not a representation of live inventory or guaranteed savings. **Illustrative barbershop example:** Five $35 haircuts total $175 in gross sales before expenses, not necessarily additional sales caused by rewards.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are experience-based rewards?

Loyalty incentives offering access to experiences or lifestyle benefits rather than only cash discounts or points.

### Is a hotel savings card cash?

No. It offers potential eligible-booking savings or credits under specific terms.

### Are getaway certificates free vacations?

Not necessarily. Mandatory fees, taxes, meals, and transportation may apply.

### Is a timeshare presentation required?

Requirements vary by specific certificate; verify the current written terms before making a claim.

### Are they better than discounts?

It depends on customer interest, redemption experience, and total program costs.
